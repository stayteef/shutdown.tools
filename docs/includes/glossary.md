---
title: Glossary
---

[ip]: /glossary/#ip "An IP (Internet Protocol) address is a unique numerical identifier assigned to every device connected to a computer network, like a home address for your device."
[lora]: /glossary/#lora "LoRa is short for "Long Range". It is a type of radio signal that sends small amounts of data over long distances using very little power."
[dns]: /glossary/#dns "DNS is the system that looks up website names. Networks rely on it, so it is often left working even when other traffic is blocked."
[vpn]: /glossary/#vpn "A VPN routes your traffic through a server elsewhere, hiding your address and encrypting your connection. It still needs some internet to work."
[metadata]: /glossary/#metadata "Metadata is hidden data about a file, such as when and where a photo was taken, stored inside the file itself."
[exif]: /glossary/#exif "Exif is metadata your camera or phone saves inside a photo, including date, device, and sometimes GPS location."
[p2p]: /glossary/#p2p "Peer-to-peer means devices connect directly to each other to share data, instead of through a central company server."
[mesh]: /glossary/#mesh "A mesh network links devices directly to each other, passing messages from device to device without the internet."
[e2ee]: /glossary/#e2ee "End-to-end encryption means only you and the person you are talking to can read the messages, not the service in between."
[ciphertext]: /glossary/#ciphertext "Ciphertext is scrambled, encrypted data. Others can see that something was sent, but not what it says."
[apk]: /glossary/#apk "An APK is the file for an Android app, like a .exe on Windows. It lets you install the app directly on your phone, without going through an app store."
